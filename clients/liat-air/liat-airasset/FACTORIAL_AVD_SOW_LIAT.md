# Factorial Performance (AVD) — Day-to-day Operating Guide

**For:** LIAT People & Culture, line managers, and employees  
**Language:** English (menu labels stay in English in Factorial)  
**Company demo:** Liat Air Revamp (demo company **195679**)  
**Last updated:** September 2026  

This is LIAT’s **front-screen operating guide** for running performance appraisals in Factorial.  
Use it when you need to create and cascade goals, run reviews (AVDs), rate competencies, coach at mid-year, and close the year with a 9-box view.

It is **not** the HRIS vendor evaluation framework. Keep that separate.

**Golden rule for LIAT goal cascade**

```text
Whole company  →  Team  →  Employees
     (parent)      (sub-goal)   (sub-goal)
```

**Correct demo example:** Whole company → Team **LIAT Flight Operations** → Employees **Tamara Joseph**  
**Wrong:** employee → employee (for example Marcus as a person → Kamala as a person). Do **not** cascade that way.

Demo goals that already show this pattern are labeled **(WCTE)** in the Goals list.

## 5. The LIAT performance year (one picture)

```text
Whole company (corporate BSC parent)     Assign to = Whole company
        ↓ cascade
Team / department scorecard              Assign to = Team
        ↓ cascade
Individual SMART goal                    Assign to = Employees
        ↓
Q1 Goal Setting & BSC Planning  →  Mid-Year Review  →  Year-End & 9-Box
   (agreements + cascade)         (progress + coaching)   (70/30 + potential)
```

**Rule:** No orphan goals. Every individual goal should link upward through **Whole company → Team → Employee** using **sub-goals**.

## 6. Goals — create, cascade, and maintain

### 6.1 Where to click

1. Left sidebar → **Performance**  
2. Tab → **Goals**  
3. Select the year (**2026**) if asked  
4. Click **+ Add goal** (or **New goal**) to create, **or** open an existing goal to add **sub-goals**

### 6.2 Understand “Assign to” (critical)

When you create a goal, Factorial asks **Assign to**. This is **not** the same as naming a manager in free text. Choose the **type** first, then the team or person.

| Assign to type | Use for | LIAT example |
|----------------|---------|--------------|
| **Whole company** | Corporate / airline-wide BSC parent | Network OTP and Safety 2026 |
| **Team** | Department scorecard | Team **LIAT Flight Operations** |
| **Employees** | One person’s SMART goal | **Tamara Joseph** |
| Legal entity / Role | Only if People & Culture specifically asks | Usually not needed for the BSC demo |

**Do this:** company → team → employee  
**Do not do this:** pick employee Marcus, then add employee Kamala as a “cascade”. That is **employee → employee** and is **wrong** for LIAT’s BSC model.

### 6.3 How to create a goal (step-by-step)

1. **Performance** → **Goals** → **+ Add goal**.  
2. **Name:** start with a BSC tag, for example  
   `[Customer / Stakeholder] Maintain on-time performance on assigned routes`.  
3. **Description:** write Measure + Due date (SMART). Example:  
   `Measure: OTP >= 85% on designated flights | Due: 2026-12-31`.  
4. **Assign to:** choose the correct type (Whole company / Team / Employees), then pick the team or person.  
5. **How it’s measured:** Percentage is fine for most BSC targets (or **Based on sub-goals** for a parent that should roll up from children).  
6. Set **Target value** (and periods if shown) → **Save**.

More help: [About Goals](https://help.factorialhr.com/en_US/about-the-goals-functionality) · [Cascading goals](https://help.factorialhr.com/en_US/cascading-goals-parent-goals-and-sub-goals).

### 6.4 How to cascade (parent → child) — LIAT way

Factorial does **not** cascade automatically. You build the tree on screen.

**Step A — Corporate parent**

1. Create (or open) the parent goal.  
2. Set **Assign to = Whole company**.  
3. Save.

**Step B — Team sub-goal**

1. Open that parent goal.  
2. Open the **Sub-goals** area → click **Add sub-goal** (or **+ Add sub-goal**).  
3. Set **Assign to = Team**.  
4. Select the department team (example: **LIAT Flight Operations**).  
5. Save.

**Step C — Employee sub-goal**

1. Open the **team** goal (not the corporate parent this time).  
2. Click **Add sub-goal** again.  
3. Set **Assign to = Employees**.  
4. Select the person (example: **Tamara Joseph**).  
5. Save.

**Step D — Check the chain**

1. On the company goal, you should see the team goal under **Sub-goals**.  
2. On the team goal, you should see the employee goal under **Sub-goals**.  
3. Breadcrumb / hierarchy should read: company → team → employee.

**Alternative link method:** open a lower goal → **Add parent goal** → pick the higher goal. Same result: one parent only per goal.

### 6.5 Demo cascade to open and show (WCTE)

These demo goals already exist and are labeled **(WCTE)**:

| Level | Assign to | Who / what | Goal to open |
|-------|-----------|------------|--------------|
| 1 Parent | **Whole company** | Liat Air Revamp | `[Corporate BSC / Customer] Network OTP and Safety 2026 (WCTE)` |
| 2 Sub-goal | **Team** | **LIAT Flight Operations** | `[Dept BSC / Customer] Flight Ops team OTP lift` |
| 3 Sub-goal | **Employees** | **Tamara Joseph** | `[Individual / Customer] Route OTP on assigned flights` |

**How to walk it on screen**

1. **Performance** → **Goals**.  
2. Search or open **Network OTP and Safety 2026 (WCTE)**.  
3. Click **Sub-goals** → open **Flight Ops team OTP lift**.  
4. Click **Sub-goals** again → open **Route OTP on assigned flights** (Tamara Joseph).  
5. Say out loud: “Whole company → Team → Employees — not person to person.”

### 6.6 Good practice for LIAT

1. Tag every title with one BSC perspective.  
2. Put Measure and Due in the description.  
3. Prefer **sub-goals** over duplicate unlinked goals.  
4. Managers finish cascade by end of Q1.  
5. Do not leave goals only in a Word pack — they must live in Factorial **Goals**.  
6. If a goal has no parent and no team link, treat it as an orphan and fix it.

## 7. Reviews (AVDs) — the three LIAT processes

Your account should contain these processes (names match the demo):

| Process | When | Purpose |
|---------|------|---------|
| **LIAT Q1 Goal Setting & BSC Planning 2026** | Early year | Agree SMART goals; start agreements; confirm cascade |
| **LIAT Q2-Q3 Mid-Year Review 2026** | Mid-year | Progress vs BSC; coaching; IDP update; 360° where enabled |
| **LIAT Q4 Year-End & 9-Box Review 2026** | Year-end (may start as Draft) | Final 70/30 rating + potential for 9-box |

### 7.1 Where to click (everyone)

1. **Performance** → **Reviews**.  
2. Open the LIAT process by name.  
3. Or open the task from **Inbox** (often faster day-to-day).

People & Culture only: use **Edit** on the process for types, score, features, and participants — before launch when possible.

### 7.2 Questionnaire blocks you will see

- **LIAT BSC Objectives (70%)** — rate / comment by perspective  
- **Competencies assessment** — native Factorial competencies (30% of the story)  
- **LIAT 360° Feedback** — peer input where enabled (especially Mid-Year)  
- **LIAT Coaching & IDP** — development actions  
- **Overall performance** / **potential** (Year-End)

### 7.3 Rating scale (use these labels)

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| DNM | Did Not Meet Expectations |
| PMS | Partially Met Some Expectations |
| MET | Met Expectations |
| EXC | Exceeded Expectations |
| SIG | Significantly Exceeded Expectations |

### 7.4 Weights 70 / 30

People & Culture must confirm in review settings that objectives and competencies follow LIAT’s **70% / 30%** rule. If **Custom weights** is available, set section weights accordingly. Questionnaire titles already state the 70% objectives intent.

### 7.5 Self-assessment and manager review

LIAT requires **self** and **supervisor** input.

1. Keep **Self-review** and **Manager** turned on for each LIAT process.  
2. Employee completes Self-review from **Inbox** or the process.  
3. Manager completes Manager review for each direct report.  
4. Discuss outcomes in a 1:1 before closing when policy requires it.

### 7.6 Peer review (360°)

1. Process **Edit** → **Review types** → enable **Peer review** while the process is still editable.  
2. Assign peer evaluators / groups in the UI.  
3. Prefer peers for Mid-Year and Year-End.

### 7.7 Mid-year — what to do on screen

1. Open **LIAT Q2-Q3 Mid-Year Review 2026**.  
2. Employee: update progress comments on BSC objectives and competencies.  
3. Manager: confirm progress, note risks (OTP, safety, audit), and update coaching.  
4. Complete the **Coaching & IDP** section with clear owners and dates.  
5. If peer review is on, complete or chase peer input.  
6. Submit / acknowledge per the process rules.

### 7.8 Agreements (Q1)

If goal agreements do not start from automation:

1. Open the Q1 process.  
2. **Initiate agreements** from the review UI so each participant has a clear Q1 contract.  
3. Employee and manager complete / accept the agreement tasks from **Inbox**.

### 7.9 E-signature

Where Signaturit / e-signature is licensed, enable signature on action plans or appraisal close-out so both employee and manager sign. If the toggle is locked on the demo, People & Culture documents the manual acknowledgement step for production.

More help: [About Performance reviews](https://help.factorialhr.com/en_US/about-performance-reviews).

## 8. Year-end and the 9-box

### 8.1 Complete the Year-End AVD

1. Open **LIAT Q4 Year-End & 9-Box Review 2026**.  
2. Confirm feature **Add potential score** is ON (feeds the 9-box; potential is hidden from the employee).  
3. Employee completes Self-review (objectives + competencies + comments).  
4. Manager rates performance (70% objectives / 30% competencies story) and enters **potential**.  
5. People & Culture + line managers calibrate in a closed session.  
6. Use outcomes for succession, development, and compensation discussions.

### 8.2 9-box access rule

Access to 9-box / Talent analytics views should stay limited to **HR + direct line managers**.

More help: [About Talent analytics / 9-box](https://help.factorialhr.com/en_US/about-the-9-box-grid-in-competencies).

## 12. Step-by-step — People & Culture checklist (first launch)

1. Confirm legal entity, location (VC Bird), teams, and managers.  
2. Confirm the three LIAT review processes exist and participants are correct.  
3. Confirm rating scale labels (DNM–SIG).  
4. Confirm Self-review + Manager (+ Peer where required).  
5. Confirm competencies assessments enabled.  
6. Confirm Q4 potential score (9-box) enabled.  
7. Create **corporate parent goals** (**Assign to = Whole company**), then cascade with **Team** and **Employees** sub-goals.  
8. Spot-check the **(WCTE)** demo chain: company → LIAT Flight Operations → Tamara Joseph.  
9. Publish announcement + assign manager trainings.  
10. Upload / confirm this PDF in **Documents**.  
11. Launch / start agreements for Q1.

## 13. Step-by-step — employee completing an AVD

1. Open **Inbox** (or **Performance → Reviews**).  
2. Open your LIAT review task.  
3. Complete **Self-review**: BSC objectives, competencies, comments.  
4. Open **Performance → Goals** and confirm your goals are current and linked upward (team / company), not orphans.  
5. Add coaching / IDP notes when the form asks.  
6. Submit. Wait for manager review and any signature / acknowledgement step.

---

## 14. Step-by-step — manager completing an AVD

1. Open your team’s LIAT review from **Inbox** or the process.  
2. For each report: open **Goals** and check cascade links (team under company; person under team).  
3. Complete **Manager** questionnaire and competency ratings.  
4. At Mid-Year: update IDPs and coaching notes with owners and dates.  
5. At Year-End: complete overall rating and **potential** (for 9-box).  
6. Discuss outcomes in 1:1; close the review / e-sign if required.

### Manager cascade checklist (Q1)

1. Open the corporate parent that applies to your area.  
2. Confirm your **Team** sub-goal exists (**Assign to = Team**).  
3. Add / confirm each report’s **Employees** sub-goal.  
4. Do **not** assign the department goal to yourself as an employee and then cascade person-to-person.

## 17. Quick troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
|---------|-------------|
| Goal has no link to company strategy | Open the goal → **Add parent goal** → pick the team or company parent |
| Cascade looks like CEO person → manager person | Recreate as Whole company → Team → Employees (see §6.4) |
| Cannot find a review task | Check **Inbox**, then **Performance → Reviews** |
| Competencies missing in the form | Ask HR to check competency assignment for the job / process |
| Cannot see 9-box | Confirm Q4 **Add potential score** is on and your role is HR or line manager |
| Cannot find this PDF | **Documents** → company space → filename above; ask HR if missing |
