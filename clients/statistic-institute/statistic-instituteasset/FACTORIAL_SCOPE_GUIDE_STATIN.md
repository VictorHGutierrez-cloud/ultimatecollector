## 1. What this manual is for

This is STATIN’s **user guide** for Factorial within the processes you run today:

- Organisation (Divisions, Units, roles)
- The five staffing processes: permanent recruitment, appointment, job letters, special project recruitment, transfers/promotions
- Leave (vacation and other absences)
- Competencies (GoJ Core and STATIN Technical)
- Performance Management System (Individual Work Plans, coaching, year-end appraisal)
- Training linked to performance
- Company and employee documents

Factorial is your **system of record** for people, jobs, leave, performance cycles, hiring activity and HR documents. Some STATIN governance steps (for example IRHC approval or Ministry vacancy control) continue **outside** Factorial and are noted in each chapter.

For product detail beyond this manual, use the [Factorial Help Center](https://help.factorialhr.com/en_US).

## 5. Leave (vacation and other absences)

### Leave types available for STATIN

In **Time off**, use the SIJ leave types configured for your organisation, including:

- SIJ Vacation Leave  
- SIJ Sick Leave  
- SIJ Maternity Leave  
- SIJ Study Leave  
- SIJ Compassionate Leave  
- SIJ Special Leave  

### Vacation entitlement bands

Vacation policies follow STATIN entitlement bands. Your own band and balance appear on your Time off screen.

| Band | Days per year | Carry-over ceiling (3 years) |
|------|---------------|------------------------------|
| Base | 15 | 45 |
| Intermediate | 20 | 60 |
| 15–25 years’ service | 21 | 63 |
| Senior | 25 | 75 |

### How balances work (in simple terms)

- Leave is counted in **working days** (Monday–Friday).  
- Public holidays do **not** reduce your vacation entitlement.  
- Entitlement can accrue over the year (**prorata**).  
- Unused days can **carry over** within the rules of your policy (up to about 36 months, capped at three times the annual entitlement).  
- Half-day rounding may apply depending on policy settings.  

HR can also make a **manual balance adjustment** when a special case requires it (for example recall of leave).

### Employee — request leave

1. Sidebar → **Time off**  
2. Click **Add time off**  
3. Choose the leave type (for example **SIJ Vacation Leave**)  
4. Select dates (and half days if offered)  
5. Attach a document if the leave type requires it  
6. Submit — your approver is notified  

Until approval, the day may not leave your available balance (depending on how the allowance is set).

### Manager / HR — approve leave

1. Open **Inbox** → **Requests**, or  
2. Open the employee’s profile → **Time off**  
3. **Approve** or **Reject** the request  

HR administrators maintain types, policies and allowances under:

**Settings → Time → Time off**

### What stays with STATIN process (not fully automatic in Factorial)

1. A strictly **daily** accrual formula (entitlement ÷ days in the year) is represented through Factorial’s accrual settings; HR monitors balances for edge cases.  
2. Pausing accrual during long absences (for example after many consecutive leave days) may need HR review and adjustment.  
3. Returning days after a leave **recall** is done with a manual allowance adjustment.  

More help: [Absences & approvals](https://help.factorialhr.com/en_US/absences-approvals) · [Time off settings](https://help.factorialhr.com/en_US/time-off-settings) · [How to request and assign time off](https://help.factorialhr.com/en_US/absences-approvals/how-to-request-and-assign-time-off)

- **60%** measurable performance targets

### Good practice

## 7. Performance Management System (PMS)

### How STATIN’s cycle appears in Factorial

| Process in Factorial | What it is for |
|----------------------|----------------|
| **STATIN FY 2025-26 Year-End Appraisal** | Close the financial year: score targets and competencies, record band, development actions, accept or wish to appeal |
| **STATIN FY 2025-26 Coaching Reviews** | Record the coaching conversations required during the year (policy: at least three formal conversations before year-end) |
| **STATIN FY 2026-27 Goal Setting and Individual Work Plan** | Agree next-year targets and key outputs (EPM Form Sections 1–4), cascaded from Division / Unit plans |

### Target rating scale

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 5 — Exceeded | 100%+ of target |
| 4 — Met all | 91–100% |
| 3 — Met most | 71–90% |
| 2 — Met some | 61–70% |
| 1 — Met few | 1–60% |
| 0 — Met none | 0% |

### Performance bands (final guidance)

| Band | Range |
|------|-------|
| Superior | 100% |
| Very Good | 91–99% |
| Good | 80–90% |
| Satisfactory | 75–79% |
| Unsatisfactory | Below 75% — Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) or development path |

### Employee — complete your part

1. Sidebar → **Performance**  
2. Open the process assigned to you (Year-End, Coaching, or IWP)  
3. Complete **self-assessment** where required  
4. Add evidence / comments on targets  
5. Complete competency ratings if asked  
6. Submit your responses  

### Manager — coach and appraise

1. Open the same process for your direct reports  
2. Review their targets and evidence  
3. Complete manager ratings and comments  
4. For coaching reviews, capture conversation notes and development actions  
5. For year-end, record the indicative band and next steps (including PIP where score is below 75%)  
6. Recommend incentives only where STATIN policy allows — Factorial records the recommendation; payroll action stays with HR / Finance processes  

### HR — run the cycle

1. Confirm participants on each process  
2. Monitor completion  
3. Support appeals through STATIN’s grievance route (Factorial stores the “wish to appeal” signal; the formal appeal is a STATIN HR process)  
4. Use **Talent analytics** / review analytics where available to see participation and trends  

### What Factorial does not replace

1. The exact **60% / 40%** arithmetic is guidance you apply in the appraisal — Factorial does not push it automatically into payroll.  
2. Increment, promotion or incentive **payment** remains an HR / Finance decision.  
3. Formal **appeals** follow STATIN grievance (and labour law if escalated).  
4. Division / Unit Word templates can stay on STATIN’s intranet; Factorial holds the individual cycle, scores and goals.  

More help: [About Performance reviews](https://help.factorialhr.com/en_US/about-performance-reviews) · [Performance review](https://help.factorialhr.com/en_US/performance-review) · [About the Goals functionality](https://help.factorialhr.com/en_US/about-the-goals-functionality)

## 8.1 Recruitment and Selection (permanent posts)

This is the standard path to fill a permanent vacancy.

### Phase 1 — Workforce need identified

**What STATIN does**

1. The Division / Unit identifies a vacancy.  
2. The **Director of the Division** submits a request to the **Staffing Unit** to fill the vacancy.  
3. Approval is obtained from the **Director, Corporate Services Division** and the **Human Resources Manager**.  
4. The **Personnel Officer** prepares and delivers a letter to the **Post Operations Committee (POC) / Ministry of Finance and the Public Service** for approval to fill the vacant post.  

**In Factorial**

1. Staffing Unit opens **Recruitment → Jobs** and creates a job in **Draft** (title, Division/team, description placeholder).  
2. Upload or attach the Division request and internal approvals under **Documents** (company internal), linked to the job name.  
3. Add a note on the job: “Awaiting POC / MoFPS approval”.  

**Outside Factorial**

- POC / MoFPS letter and formal Ministry approval.  
- Committee correspondence that is not stored as a company document.

---

### Phase 2 — Job advertisement

**What STATIN does**

1. Staffing Unit prepares the vacancy advertisement:  
   - Create the **Job Description** for the post.  
   - Create a **job advertisement** derived from the Job Description.  
   - **Director of Corporate Services** or **HR Manager** approves the advertisement.  
   - Advertisement details go to the **Communication and Marketing Unit** for social media.  
2. Corporate Services or HR decides whether the post is advertised **internally**, **externally**, or both (example: Editor Coders are temporary posts advertised internally).  
3. Staffing Unit receives job applications.  

**In Factorial**

1. Open the draft job → paste / attach the approved Job Description and advert text.  
2. Set application requirements (CV, cover letter, phone, etc.).  
3. Choose visibility: keep **Draft** for internal-only preparation, or **Publish** for the career site / external channel STATIN uses.  
4. Tag or title clearly if the post is **internal only**.  
5. Applications arrive as **Candidates / Applications** on that job.  

**Outside Factorial**

- Social media production by Communication and Marketing.  
- Email blasts that are not via Factorial careers (you can still register applicants manually in Recruitment).

---

### Phase 3 — Screening and shortlisting

**What STATIN does**

1. Staffing Unit reviews all applications.  
2. Staffing Unit shortlists using the **short list matrix** template.  
   - Pass mark is normally **70%**.  
   - Occasionally lowered to **60%** when that better reflects the strongest candidate pool.  
3. Staffing Unit prepares the **interview schedule**.  

**In Factorial**

1. Open the job → **Candidates / Applications**.  
2. Move people through the **hiring pipeline** stages (for example: New → Screening → Shortlisted → Interview).  
3. Attach the completed short list matrix and scores to the job or candidate.  
4. Reject or archive candidates who do not meet the matrix.  
5. Record interview dates in notes or schedule fields on the candidate.  

More help: [About the hiring pipeline](https://help.factorialhr.com/en_US/about-the-hiring-pipeline) · [How to manage job applications](https://help.factorialhr.com/en_US/how-to-manage-job-applications-)

---

### Phase 4 — Interview and assessment

**What STATIN does**

1. Staffing Unit writes to **Training and Development** to administer tests (if required) and to arrange lunch for panellists.  
2. For non–head office posts: request interview locations; request a STATIN vehicle for panellists; write to **General Services** for refreshments at out-of-town venues.  
3. For Head Office: write to **General Services** for refreshments and parking for external panellists and candidates.  
4. Interviews are conducted by Staffing Unit, the Supervisor or Division Director, plus an **external panellist**.  
5. Job interview tests are administered before or on interview day; scores are shared with panellists.  
6. Interview package for each panellist: resume, job advertisement, job description, rating sheet.  
7. Complete the **Interview Score Summary** (competency totals; identify the highest score).  
8. Panel submits recommendations to Staffing Unit by **Memo**, with Interview Summary attached.  
9. Staffing Unit requests a token/gift for the external panellist from Communication and Marketing.  

**In Factorial**

1. Keep shortlisted candidates in an **Interview** pipeline stage.  
2. Attach to each candidate (or to the job folder): resume, advert, JD, rating sheet, test scores, Interview Score Summary, panel Memo.  
3. Record panel outcome and ranking in candidate notes.  
4. Advance the preferred candidate; mark others as rejected / talent pool as STATIN policy allows.  

**Outside Factorial**

- Venue, transport, lunch, parking and panellist tokens (Training, General Services, Marketing).  
- Physical panel meeting logistics.

---

### Phase 5 — Selection and approval

**What STATIN does**

1. Background check for the successful candidate(s):  
   - **Reference Checklist** completed by the candidate’s immediate supervisor (where applicable).  
   - Recommendation from a notary public (for example Justice of the Peace / Police) or a manager.  
2. Staffing Unit submits a request to the **Institutional Resilience and Human Capital (IRHC) Committee** (Director General chairs) for approval of the successful candidate.  
3. Obtain **final IRHC approval**.  

**In Factorial**

1. Move the chosen candidate to a stage such as **Offer pending / IRHC**.  
2. Upload Reference Checklist, notary/manager recommendation, and IRHC request/approval memos to **Documents** (and link to the candidate/job).  
3. Do not issue the formal offer until IRHC approval is on file.  

**Outside Factorial**

- IRHC meeting, minutes and formal committee decision.

---

### Phase 6 — Recruitment folder

**What STATIN does**

There is a **recruitment folder for each post**. It must contain:

- Panellist interview rating sheets  
- Candidate qualifications  
- Photocopied identification  
- Resume and cover letter  
- Completed short-listing document  
- Interview score summary  
- Job advertisement  
- Interview schedule  
- Short list matrix  
- Approval for the person selected  

**In Factorial**

1. Create (or use) a **company internal Documents** folder named for the post (for example `Recruitment — [Post title] — [Year]`).  
2. Upload every artefact listed above.  
3. Keep the same set on the job/candidate where Factorial allows attachments, so Staffing Unit has one operational view.  

---

### Phase 7 — Job offer

**What STATIN does**

1. Staffing Unit contacts the successful candidate.  
2. Staffing Unit issues **two offer letters** for the candidate to review terms and conditions.  
3. On acceptance: the candidate keeps one signed copy; the other signed copy goes in the candidate’s folder in the HR cabinet (and digitally in Factorial).  

**In Factorial**

1. Use the **Offer letter** flow on the candidate / job.  
2. Store both the issued and signed copies in **Documents** (job folder and, after hire, employee documents).  
3. Use **e-signature** when STATIN requires electronic signature.  

More help: [About the Offer letter](https://help.factorialhr.com/en_US/about-the-offer-letter)

---

### Phase 8 — Employment commencement

**What STATIN does**

1. Candidate reports for duty.  
2. Educational documents: collection may start on interview day and is completed on orientation day.  
3. Orientation and onboarding with the new employee.  
4. Staffing Unit prepares the **Employment of staff form**; approvals by HR Manager or Director of Corporate Services, immediate Supervisor, and **Finance Unit**.  
5. Form goes to Finance to process salary.  

**In Factorial**

1. Convert / hire the candidate to an **employee**.  
2. Assign team, manager and job in **Organization**.  
3. Run **onboarding** checklist / tasks.  
4. Upload educational documents and the Employment of staff form (with approvals) to employee **Documents**.  
5. Notify Finance using your STATIN process; keep the approved form in Documents as the audit trail.  

More help: [Onboarding workflow](https://help.factorialhr.com/en_US/onboarding-workflow) · [What information is transferred when converting a candidate?](https://help.factorialhr.com/en_US/what-information-is-transferred-when-converting-a-candidate)

## 8.2 Appointment of Staff Process

Used after someone has joined, to move from **probation** to **confirmed appointment**.

### Phase 1 — Probationary period

**What STATIN does**

1. Employee serves a **six (6) month** probation period.  
2. In the **4th or 5th month**, Staffing Unit notifies the supervisor that the **probationary report** must be completed.  
3. Supervisor monitors performance; performance reviews are conducted.  

**In Factorial**

1. Record probation end date / notes on the employee profile (custom field or contract notes as STATIN configures).  
2. Create a **Task** for the supervisor in month 4–5: “Complete probationary report”.  
3. Use **Performance** coaching or a short review if STATIN links probation to the PMS cycle.  

### Phase 2 — Probation assessment

**What STATIN does**

1. Supervisor submits the probation evaluation report.  
2. **HR Manager** reviews the recommendation.  

**In Factorial**

1. Upload the probation evaluation report to the employee’s **Documents**.  
2. HR Manager records recommendation in notes / Tasks completion.  

### Phase 3 — Medical examination

**What STATIN does**

1. Employee receives a referral for medical examination.  
2. Employee completes the medical assessment.  
3. Medical report is submitted to Human Resources.  
4. Staffing Unit submits a request to **IRHC** for approval for appointment.  

**In Factorial**

1. Store referral and medical report in employee **Documents** (restricted permissions).  
2. Attach IRHC appointment request to the same pack.  

**Outside Factorial:** medical clinic process and IRHC decision meeting.

### Phase 4 — Decision

**What STATIN does**

- If **satisfactory:** IRHC recommends appointment / confirmation.  
- If **unsatisfactory:** IRHC extends probation or terminates employment.  

**In Factorial**

1. Update employee status / probation dates if extended.  
2. If terminating, follow STATIN offboarding and Contracts / terminations practice in Factorial.  
3. File IRHC decision letter in Documents.  

### Phase 5 — Confirmation of appointment

**What STATIN does**

1. Staffing Unit prepares the **Appointment / Confirmation Letter**.  
2. Approval obtained from **HR Manager**.  
3. Employee receives the letter.  
4. Employee is referred to the **Leave and Benefits Unit** for benefits processes.  

**In Factorial**

1. Generate or upload the Confirmation Letter (Document templates / e-signature as configured).  
2. Place a signed copy on the employee profile.  
3. Confirm leave policy assignment under **Time off** so Leave and Benefits can continue setup.

## 8.3 Job Verification / Request for Job Letter

### Phase 1 — Request received

**What STATIN does:** Employee submits the request (typically by email). HR logs the request.  

**In Factorial:** Log as a Task / Inbox item for Personnel; note the requesting institution if the letter goes to a third party.

### Phase 2 — Verification

**What STATIN does:** Confirm employee status; verify employment information.  

**In Factorial:** Check **Organization** profile (active status, job title, dates, team, manager).

### Phase 3 — Letter preparation

**What STATIN does:** Prepare the employment verification / job letter.  

**In Factorial:** Use a **Document template** for job letters; draft the letter and save under employee Documents.

### Phase 4 — Approval

**What STATIN does:** Review and approval by Director Corporate Services / HR Manager / Senior Personnel Officer and Personnel Officer (as your signature matrix requires).  

**In Factorial:** Route for approval via e-signature or upload the wet-ink signed PDF once approved.

### Phase 5 — Issuance

**What STATIN does:** Hand-deliver or email to the employee, or email to the requesting institution.  

**In Factorial:** Keep the final issued copy on the employee file; note date and recipient in comments.

## 8.4 Special Project Recruitment Process

For **Census, Labour Force Survey, Household Surveys, IES** and similar field / contract programmes.

### Phase 1 — Project approval

**What STATIN does**

1. Project prepared by **Director of Field Services**.  
2. Approved by **Director of Corporate Services** or **HR Manager**.  
3. Staffing Unit identifies staffing requirements.  

**In Factorial:** File project approval memo in Documents; create draft temporary job(s) for expected roles (Interviewers, Field Supervisors, HQ Editor Coders, other project staff).

### Phase 2 — Consultation with project team

**What STATIN does**

1. HR Manager meets the Project Manager.  
2. Agree numbers for Interviewers, Field Supervisors, HQ Editor Coders, other staff.  
3. Receive a memorandum with required field staff numbers and approval.  

**In Factorial:** Update job openings / headcount notes on each temporary job; attach the staffing memo.

### Phase 3 — Advertisement

**What STATIN does**

1. Develop advertisements (same standards as permanent recruitment).  
2. Publish recruitment notices by **email to Field Services Supervisors** so persons can be identified and shortlisted.  

**In Factorial:** Publish temporary job postings; Staffing can also add candidates identified by Field Supervisors manually.

### Phase 4 — Application collection

**What STATIN does:** Staffing Unit receives applications/resumes and creates an **application database**.  

**In Factorial:** The job’s **Candidates / Applications** list is the application database. Import or add candidates as they arrive.

### Phase 5 — Screening

**What STATIN does**

1. Review applications.  
2. Shortlist with a **rating sheet and questionnaire**; candidates evaluated with a **reading package**.  
3. Pass mark **70%** (sometimes **60%**).  
4. Contact candidates; receive and verify copies of **TRN**, **NIS** and identification.  

**In Factorial:** Pipeline stages for screening; attach rating sheets and reading-package results; upload TRN/NIS/ID copies to the candidate (move to employee Documents after hire).

### Phase 6 — Engagement (logistics)

**What STATIN does**

1. Write to Training and Development for lunch and accommodation (if required).  
2. Write to General Services to secure interview venues (Field Offices, churches, other locations).  

**In Factorial:** Optional logistics checklist as Tasks. Venue booking itself stays outside Factorial.

### Phase 7 — Interview and selection

**What STATIN does**

Same interview standards as permanent recruitment: panel (Staffing, Supervisor/Director, external panellist), tests, interview package (resume, advert, JD, rating sheet), Interview Score Summary, Memo to Staffing, token for external panellist.  

**In Factorial:** Same as Phase 4 of permanent recruitment — pipeline, attachments, ranking notes.

### Phase 8 — Training

**What STATIN does:** Submit the final training participant list to process owners for training.  

**In Factorial:** Enrol selected people in the relevant **Training** course/group; attach the participant list in Documents.

### Phase 9 — Onboarding

**What STATIN does**

1. Arrange lunch/accommodation and onboarding venues (Training / General Services).  
2. Contact successful candidates with onboarding location and reporting time.  
3. Collect and verify: birth certificate, **TRN**, **NIS**, banking information, government ID, passport-sized photograph.  
4. Facilitate acceptance and signing of: employment contract, **Oath of Secrecy**, Declaration Form, Attestation Form, Temporary Application Form, Job Description, Employee Incident Report Form.  
5. HR Manager reviews the accepted offer letter.  
6. Enter contract staff information into the **ProJems** database.  

**In Factorial**

1. Hire / create temporary employees; assign Field Services (or project) team.  
2. Upload the full document pack to employee **Documents**.  
3. Use e-signature where STATIN allows.  
4. Complete onboarding Tasks.  

**Outside Factorial:** ProJems data entry (you may keep an export or checklist copy in Documents).

### Phase 10 — Payments

**What STATIN does:** Submit **ProJems Reports** to Finance and Accounting for training expenses and other emoluments; HR Manager approves the report.  

**In Factorial:** File the approved report copy in Documents for audit. Payment processing remains in Finance / ProJems.

## 8.5 Staff Transfer / Promotion Process

### Competitive process

Follows the same spine as permanent **Recruitment and Selection**:

1. Workforce need identified (Division request → Corporate Services + HR → POC/MoFPS letter).  
2. Job advertisement (JD → advert → approval → Marketing; internal and/or external).  
3. Screening and shortlisting (matrix; 70% / sometimes 60%; interview schedule).  
4. Interview and assessment (tests, panel pack, score summary, Memo, panellist token).  
5. Selection and approval (background checks → IRHC).  
6. Job offer (two letters; signed copies filed).  
7. Employment commencement / move into the new role.  

**In Factorial (extra steps after competitive win)**

1. Run the vacancy in **Recruitment** (internal candidates apply to the posting).  
2. After IRHC and acceptance: update the person’s **job**, **team** and **manager** in Organization.  
3. Refresh Job catalog assignment and competencies if the new level differs.  
4. File promotion / transfer letter in employee Documents.  

### Internal process (non-competitive)

**What STATIN does**

1. Vacancy identified.  
2. Employee is recommended or applies.  
3. Assessment conducted.  
4. Approval received from **IRHC**.  
5. New appointment / promotion letter issued by **HR Manager**.  
6. HR records updated.  

**In Factorial**

1. Record recommendation / assessment notes on the employee (and optional internal job draft if you still want a paper trail).  
2. File IRHC approval and promotion letter in Documents.  
3. Update job, team, manager and leave/benefits implications on the profile.  
4. No full public advertisement is required for this path — keep the audit pack complete anyway.

## 11. Documents

### Spaces you will see

| Space | Who it is for |
|-------|----------------|
| **Company internal** documents | HR and authorised staff — policies, guides, recruitment packs, committee letters |
| **Employee documents** | Files on a person’s profile (contracts, certificates, personal letters) |

### Good practice for STATIN

1. Keep recruitment folders and IRHC / offer letters in **Documents** with clear names.  
2. Assign personal files to the employee when they belong on the profile.  
3. Use **e-signature** when STATIN requires a signed electronic copy.  
4. Control access with **permission groups** so sensitive HR files stay restricted.

## 12. Permissions (who can see and do what)

Administrators configure **permission groups** under **Settings → Permissions**.

Typical pattern for STATIN:

| Group idea | Access |
|------------|--------|
| Employees | Own profile, own leave requests, own reviews and documents |
| Managers | Team leave approvals, team performance, limited people data |
| HR / Staffing | Recruitment, leave admin, documents, broader employee data |
| Administrators | Settings, policies, permission groups |

Ask HR if you cannot see a screen you need — it is usually a permission, not a missing feature.

More help: [Short guide to Permissions](https://help.factorialhr.com/en_US/short-guide-to-permissions)
