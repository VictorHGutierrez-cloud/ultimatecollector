**Language:** English  
**Focus:** Performance Management System (PMS) — Individual Work Plans, coaching, year-end appraisal

1. Log in to the Factorial demo for company **191864**.

## Minute 0–1 — Context

**Say:**

> “STATIN’s Performance Management System cascades from Government priorities to the Strategic Plan, Operational Plan, Division Plan, Unit Plan, then each employee’s **Individual Work Plan**.  
> Final rating guidance is **60% measurable targets / 40% GoJ core and technical competencies**.  
> Bands: Superior 100%, Very Good 91–99%, Good 80–90%, Satisfactory 75–79%, **Unsatisfactory below 75%** — that triggers a PIP or development path.”

**Show (optional):** Teams starting with **STATIN** (ODG, Corporate Services, Field Services, Economic and Social Statistics, Human Resources).

---

## Minute 1–3 — Close the year (Year-End Appraisal)

1. Open **Performance** → **STATIN FY 2025-26 Year-End Appraisal**.
2. Show participants (the four demo people).
3. Open the questionnaire sections:
   - **STATIN Performance Targets (60%)**
   - **STATIN Coaching, Development & Outcomes** (band, accept/appeal, Section 10 action)
   - **Native Competencies assessment** (40%) — appears after you import the GoJ Excel files (see `COMPETENCIES_IMPORT_README.md`)
4. Open **Hellen Howard** (or paste goals from the goals pack):
   - Embargoed releases within 1 hour  
   - Zero confidentiality breaches  
   - Records compliance  

**Say:**

> “Hellen is our Good (~86%) story — the same pattern as the completed sample in STATIN’s PMS guidelines. Targets plus competencies, evidence, then a clear band.”

5. Contrast with **Daisy Dawson**:

**Say:**

> “Daisy is below 75%. Policy says underperformance: if it is a competence gap, we support with training/PIP; if unwillingness, we follow STATIN policy. Factorial captures the score and the development plan — HR owns the formal PIP outcome.”

6. Mention **Laura Lewis** briefly as Very Good / Superior → incentive recommendation (manager recommendation, not auto-payroll).

- **STATIN PMS Guidelines for Managers**
- **STATIN Performance Coaching**

## Minute 5–6 — Open next year (IWP)
